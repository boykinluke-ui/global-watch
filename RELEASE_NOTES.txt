DATA AND SYNC
• The published research snapshot is rebuilt by GitHub Actions: GDELT and configured UN Geneva feeds are attempted daily; World Bank and UNHCR are attempted weekly. A scheduled attempt does not mean every source has new data.
• Personal watchlists, prayer notes, checklists, profile drafts and story drafts remain local to one browser/device. They do not sync across accounts or visitors.
• This GitHub Pages edition has no member accounts, shared profile directory, public post feed, moderation queue, or media-upload service.

LIMITATIONS
The application is public and read-only for hosted data. Scheduled GitHub runs can be delayed and may be disabled after long repository inactivity. The site is not a complete country assessment or a live mission-opening feed. Review source definitions, dates, safety guidance, local leadership, safeguarding and data reuse terms before acting or republishing.
